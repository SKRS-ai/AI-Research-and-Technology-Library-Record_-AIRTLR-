The Authorship Equation

The NMMMB Metric is the proprietary numerical calculation used to determine the ratio of Human vs. Machine input. To qualify for Bureau Certification, an asset must exceed the 51% Human Input threshold.

H_i = (M_p + M_d + M_l) / (N_b) > 0.51

Weight Factors:

  • M_p: Manual Prompt Iteration (Human Intent)
  • M_d: Digital Transformation (Over-painting/Layering)
  • M_l: Logical Architecture (Metastructure Design)
  • N_b: Neural Baseline (Raw AI Output)
HUMAN: 51%+ (CERTIFIED)
MACHINE: 49%- (NON-IP)
